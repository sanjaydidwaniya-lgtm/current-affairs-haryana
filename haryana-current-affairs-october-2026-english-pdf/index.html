1. CM and Cabinet

1.1 Cabinet exempts ex-Agniveers from all examinations for covered Group C posts

Date: 10 October 2026

  • The Haryana Cabinet, chaired by Chief Minister Nayab Singh Saini, amended the ex-Agniveer employment policy on 10 October 2026.
  • Ex-Agniveers are exempt from all examinations prescribed for recruitment to the Group C posts covered under the policy.
  • They will be considered for direct appointment against advertised posts, subject to reservation and other applicable provisions.
  • They are also exempt from the Physical Screening Test and Physical Measurement Test for Wildlife Guard, Constable in the India Reserve Battalion, Constable in the State Disaster Response Force and Fire Operator-cum-Driver.
Exam angle: Remember: exam exemption for Group C; PST and PMT exemption for four named posts.

1.2 Cabinet continues 6th State Finance Commission devolution and doubles media welfare cover

Date: 10 October 2026

  • The same Cabinet meeting of 10 October 2026 also took decisions on local body finance and media welfare.
  • It approved the continuation of the 6th State Finance Commission recommendations for fund devolution to rural and urban local bodies from 2026-27.
  • The arrangement stays until the next State Finance Commission report arrives, because the 7th State Finance Commission had not yet submitted its report.
  • It raised the maximum financial assistance under the Haryana Media Personnel Welfare Fund scheme from Rs 2.5 lakh to Rs 5 lakh.
Exam angle: State Finance Commission is set up under Article 243-I and 243-Y. Remember: 6th continues, 7th report pending.

1.3 Saini government completes two years of its current term

Date: 17 October 2026

  • Nayab Singh Saini took oath for his current term on 17 October 2024, so the government completed two years on 17 October 2026.
  • The Chief Minister marked the occasion with a press conference in Chandigarh.
  • He said the visits to Sweden and Singapore brought investment proposals worth Rs 9,500 crore in total.
  • Rs 9,500 crore is the sum of about Rs 5,500 crore from Singapore and Rs 4,000 crore from Sweden.
Exam angle: Saini is the Chief Minister in his second stint since 17 October 2024. Overseas proposals are not the same as signed investment.

3. Schemes and Policies

3.1 Deen Dayal Lado Lakshmi Yojana amended: income limit raised to Rs 1.80 lakh

Date: 10 October 2026

  • The Cabinet raised the annual family income limit from Rs 1 lakh to Rs 1.80 lakh.
  • The option to choose a lower monthly amount was removed. Every beneficiary gets Rs 2,100 a month.
  • For new beneficiaries the first instalment goes fully to the savings account. From the second month Rs 1,100 goes to the account and Rs 1,000 goes to a government Recurring Deposit or Fixed Deposit.
  • Face authentication (liveness check) through the Lado Lakshmi app is now needed once every quarter instead of every month.
  • The amendments take effect on 1 November 2026. More than 10 lakh women receive the benefit.
Exam angle: Remember the split: Rs 2,100 = Rs 1,100 cash + Rs 1,000 deposit. Liveness check is quarterly.

3.2 Beekeeping brought under the Mukhyamantri Bagwani Bima Yojana

Date: 24 October 2026

  • The Horticulture Department extended the Mukhyamantri Bagwani Bima Yojana to cover beekeepers.
  • The premium is Rs 50 each per bee colony and per bee box. The insured amount is Rs 1,250 each.
  • One beekeeper can insure up to 250 colonies and 250 boxes. The maximum cover is Rs 6.25 lakh (Rs 3,12,500 each for colonies and boxes).
  • Cover applies to losses from floods, fire, natural calamities and road accidents during colony migration.
Exam angle: Check: 250 x Rs 1,250 = Rs 3,12,500; two such amounts make Rs 6.25 lakh.

4. Economy, Industry, MSME and Budget

4.1 Startup Haryana Seva Sankalp programme launched at Kurukshetra University

Date: 2 October 2026

  • Chief Minister Nayab Singh Saini launched the Startup Haryana Seva Sankalp Programme at Kurukshetra University on 2 October 2026.
  • He launched it at the Vichar Se Vistar Tak programme held on the birth anniversaries of Mahatma Gandhi and Lal Bahadur Shastri.
  • He said no worthy idea should be left behind for lack of resources.
  • The programme aims to take entrepreneurship to the grassroots across the state.
Exam angle: Launch place: Kurukshetra University. Launch day: 2 October.

4.2 Singapore visit brings investment proposals of about Rs 5,500 crore

Date: 6 October 2026

  • Chief Minister Saini concluded his Singapore visit on 6 October 2026.
  • Haryana received investment proposals worth about Rs 5,500 crore.
  • The proposed MoUs cover health and wellness (Rs 3,000 crore), water treatment and management (Rs 1,500 crore), and education and skill development (Rs 1,000 crore).
  • Companies and institutions agreed to move towards Memoranda of Understanding.
Exam angle: Check: 3,000 + 1,500 + 1,000 = 5,500. These are proposals, not completed investment.

4.3 Sweden visit opens way for Rs 4,000 crore investment

Date: 17 October 2026

  • Chief Minister Saini said his three-day Sweden visit opened the way for Rs 4,000 crore investment in Haryana.
  • He gave the details on 17 October 2026 at a press conference in Chandigarh.
  • The investment is expected to create more than 11,000 jobs.
  • The visit aimed to attract foreign investment and advanced technology to the state.
Exam angle: Sweden: Rs 4,000 crore and more than 11,000 jobs. Singapore: about Rs 5,500 crore.

5. Agriculture, MSP and Procurement

5.1 Kharif paddy procurement 2026-27 gathers pace in Haryana mandis

Date: 12 and 15 October 2026

  • Procurement began on 29 September 2026 and runs until 15 November through 249 designated mandis.
  • By 12 October, Haryana bought 11.90 lakh metric tonnes out of 18.06 lakh metric tonnes of arrivals, paying Rs 758 crore to 48,152 farmers.
  • By 15 October, arrivals were 21.27 lakh metric tonnes, purchases 15.52 lakh metric tonnes and lifting 7.69 lakh metric tonnes. Payment reached Rs 1,221 crore.
  • Against the Government of India target of 58 lakh metric tonnes, the state had reached about 27 per cent.
  • The MSP for 2026-27 is Rs 2,441 a quintal for common paddy and Rs 2,461 for Grade A, fixed in May 2026.
Exam angle: Remember: start 29 September, end 15 November, 249 mandis, target 58 lakh metric tonnes.

5.2 Paddy above the prescribed average yield to be bought at MSP

Date: 18 October 2026

  • Agriculture Minister Shyam Singh Rana announced on 18 October 2026 that paddy above the prescribed average yield would also be procured.
  • Farmers with a higher than average yield submit written claims to the market committee secretary.
  • The claims are to be resolved within 48 hours.
  • Rana inspected the grain markets of Sirsa, Dabwali, Kalanwali and Rania, where farmers and commission agents raised delayed lifting, storage space and fertiliser supply.
Exam angle: Remember: written claim to market committee secretary; resolution within 48 hours.

5.3 Khet Bachao Abhiyan-Rabi 2026 launched from Karnal district

Date: 22 October 2026

  • Union Agriculture Minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan launched the nationwide Khet Bachao Abhiyan-Rabi 2026 from Karnal district on 22 October 2026.
  • Haryana Agriculture Minister Shyam Singh Rana attended the launch.
  • The campaign runs until 30 November 2026.
  • It promotes balanced use of inputs based on soil tests, use of fields left vacant after the paddy harvest, crop residue management and organic farming.
  • A baler machine was demonstrated, and Haryana farmers were commended for fewer stubble- burning incidents.
Exam angle: Remember: launched in Karnal on 22 October; runs until 30 November.

6.1 HPSC declares final result of HCS and allied services examination

Date: 17 October 2026

  • The Haryana Public Service Commission declared the final result with allocation of service on 17 October 2026.
  • The result covers 102 posts under HCS (Executive Branch) and allied services.
  • The allocation list was released the same day.
  • The commission was led by Alok Verma until 22 October 2026 (see 7.1).

  • The Haryana Staff Selection Commission notified the final list for Advertisement No. 08/2024 (CET Group C posts) on 23 October 2026.
  • A corrigendum on 28 October 2026 revised the 23 October list.

  • Candidates had to check the revised list for their roll number and post.
Exam angle: Advertisement 08/2024; list on 23 October; revision on 28 October.

7. Administration and Appointments

7.1 HPSC Chairman Alok Verma completes his term

Date: 22 October 2026

  • Alok Verma's six-year term as HPSC Chairman ended on 22 October 2026.
  • He is a retired 1989-batch Indian Forest Service officer.
  • He took oath as Chairman in October 2020.
  • The commission was without a chairman after his exit.
Exam angle: Under Article 316 the Governor appoints the chairman; term is six years or age 62, whichever is earlier.

7.2 Satish Kumar appointed Chairman of the Haryana Public Service Commission

Date: 29 October 2026

  • The Haryana Government notified the appointment of Satish Kumar, a retired HCS officer, as HPSC Chairman by a Gazette Extraordinary notification dated 29 October 2026.
  • Governor Prof. Ashim Kumar Ghosh administered the oath of office at Lok Bhavan, Chandigarh on 29 October 2026.
  • He fills the vacancy left when Alok Verma's term ended on 22 October 2026.
  • The appointment took effect from the date he assumed charge.
Exam angle: Satish Kumar succeeded Alok Verma. The Governor appoints the chairman under Article 316.

8. Sports

8.1 Haryana honours Asian Games and Para Asian Games athletes with Rs 185 crore

Date: 24 and 30 October 2026

  • On 24 October 2026 Sports and Youth Affairs Minister of State Gaurav Gautam announced Rs 185 crore cash awards for 171 athletes of the 2026 Asian Games and Asian Para Games held in Japan.
  • The split was Rs 133 crore for 134 Asian Games athletes and Rs 52 crore for 37 para-athletes.
  • A state-level ceremony took place at Bhim Stadium, Bhiwani on 30 October with Chief Minister Saini as chief guest.
  • Haryana athletes won 32 medals at the Asian Games (14 gold, 9 silver, 9 bronze), and 31 medals including 9 gold at the Asian Para Games.
Exam angle: Check: 134 + 37 = 171 athletes; Rs 133 crore + Rs 52 crore = Rs 185 crore.

9. Culture, Tourism and Awards

9.1 Governor reviews preparations for the International Gita Mahotsav at Kurukshetra

Date: 29 October 2026

  • Governor Prof. Ashim Kumar Ghosh chaired the 83rd meeting of the Governing Body of the Kurukshetra Development Board on 29 October 2026. Chief Minister Saini attended.
  • The festival is scheduled in Kurukshetra from 5 to 25 December 2026.
  • An awareness campaign covering 182 pilgrimage sites of the 48 kos Kurukshetra region begins on 2 December, and a procession leaves Pipli on 4 December.
  • The programmes include Bhagwat Katha, Gita conferences and an exhibition marking 60 years of Haryana's formation.
Exam angle: Remember: 5 to 25 December; 182 sites; 48 kos; Governor chairs the Kurukshetra Development Board.

10. Environment, Water, Yamuna, SYL and Air Quality

10.1 Climate Trends analysis: stubble fires barely influenced Delhi air in 2024 to 2026

Date: 28 October 2026

  • On 28 October 2026 the Delhi-based research group Climate Trends released an analysis of Delhi air quality for 15 September to 23 October in 2024, 2025 and 2026.
  • It found Delhi air was barely influenced by stubble burning in Punjab and Haryana.
  • Fire events in Punjab and Haryana together (15 September to 15 October) were 193 in 2026 against 201 in 2025, a 4 per cent fall.
  • Delhi mean Air Quality Index fell from 169.3 to 142.6 (15.8 per cent), while the median rose from 135 to 154 (14.1 per cent).
Exam angle: Mean fell but median rose: fewer very bad days, higher baseline.
