1. National

1. Six New Governors Appointed

12 October 2026

  • President Droupadi Murmu appointed Governors in six states.
  • Om Prakash Mathur (till now Governor of Sikkim) became Governor of Keralam; R. V. Arlekar (Keralam) moved to Tamil Nadu.
  • Braj Mohan Ram was appointed Governor of Odisha; Dr Sudha Yadav of Karnataka; Dhal Singh Bisen of Uttarakhand.
  • Dushyant Gautam was appointed Governor of Sikkim.
Exam angle: Match each Governor with the state. Note that Mathur and Arlekar were transferred, while the other four are new appointments.

2. National Social Assistance Programme Continued

14 October 2026

  • The Union Cabinet approved continuation of the National Social Assistance Programme (NSAP) for five years, 2026-27 to 2030-31.
  • Outlay: ₹53,428 crore. The programme is renamed Pradhan Mantri Samajik Sahayata Yojana.
  • It is expected to benefit about 3.2 crore people across all States and Union Territories.
  • The benchmark disability for the disability pension is reduced from 80 per cent to 40 per cent; old-age and widow pensions continue in their existing form.
Exam angle: Remember the new name, the ₹53,428 crore outlay and the change of the disability benchmark from 80% to 40%.

3. Productivity Linked Bonus for Railway Employees

14 October 2026

  • The Cabinet approved a Productivity Linked Bonus (PLB) equal to 78 days’ wages for railway employees.
  • About 11.4 lakh employees (11,40,770) are covered.
  • The expenditure is about ₹2,010 crore (₹2,009.61 crore).
Exam angle: Remember 78 days and about 11.4 lakh employees.

4. Strategic Sale of 60.72% Stake in IDBI Bank

14 October 2026

  • The Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs (CCEA) approved the strategic sale of a 60.72 per cent stake in IDBI Bank.
  • The sale value is ₹52,878 crore; the buyer is an entity of Fairfax Financial Holdings (Canada).
  • The stake comprises 30.48% held by the Government of India and 30.24% held by the Life Insurance Corporation (LIC).
  • After the sale the Government is to retain 15% and LIC 19%.
Exam angle: Strategic disinvestment: remember the 60.72% total (30.48% + 30.24%) and the buyer, Fairfax.

8. Container Manufacturing Promotion Scheme (CMPS)

28 October 2026

  • The Union Cabinet, chaired by PM Narendra Modi, approved the Container Manufacturing Promotion Scheme; it was announced in Union Budget 2026-27.
  • Total outlay: ₹10,000 crore, running up to FY 2039-40 (a 13-year window).
  • Goal: raise annual container manufacturing capacity from about 70,000 TEUs to 7.5 lakh TEUs and cut import dependence.
  • Components: Interest Subvention Assistance ₹859 crore (3% per annum), Operational Expenditure Assistance ₹8,891 crore, Testing and Certification ₹150 crore, plus land leasing support.
Exam angle: TEU means twenty-foot equivalent unit. Remember ₹10,000 crore, FY 2039-40 and the 7.5 lakh TEU target.

9. Central Armed Police Forces (General Administration) Act, 2026 in Force

8 October 2026

  • The Ministry of Home Affairs brought the CAPF (General Administration) Act, 2026 into force on 8 October 2026.
  • It covers the CRPF, BSF, CISF, ITBP and SSB and gives one legal framework for recruitment, promotion, deputation and service conditions.
  • Quotas for Indian Police Service (IPS) officers on deputation: 100% of Director General and Special Director General posts, at least 67% of Additional Director General posts and 50% of Inspector General posts.
  • More than 3,000 serving Group-A CAPF officers have challenged the Act in the Supreme Court over promotion stagnation.
Exam angle: Know the five forces and the IPS quotas (100%, 67%, 50%). The matter is pending in the Supreme Court.

10. National Social Security Board for Gig and Platform Workers

30 October 2026

  • The Ministry of Labour and Employment notified the National Social Security Board for gig and platform workers; the notification of a 40-member board for unorganised workers accompanied it.
  • The 22-member board is chaired by the Union Labour Minister, Dr Mansukh Mandaviya.
  • It includes Central and State representatives, aggregators such as delivery and ride-hailing companies, workers’ unions and civil society.
  • The boards are set up under the Code on Social Security, 2020, which came into force on 21 November 2025.
Exam angle: Remember the Code on Social Security, 2020, the chair (Labour Minister) and the 22-member strength.
Haryana angle: The Haryana Government had announced plans for a state-level board for the social and economic security of gig workers in January 2024.

11. Calling Name Presentation (CNAP) from 1 November

Order issued in October 2026; effective 1 November 2026

  • The Department of Telecommunications (DoT) directed telecom operators to introduce Calling Name Presentation (CNAP) across India from 1 November 2026.
  • The caller’s name, as registered in the telecom operator’s KYC records, appears with the number; a name saved in the recipient’s contacts takes precedence.
  • Calls from abroad are shown as “International Call”.
  • The service is on by default on 4G and 5G networks; users with Calling Line Identification Restriction (CLIR) are exempt.
Exam angle: CNAP uses telecom KYC data, not crowd-sourced apps. Remember the 1 November start and the DoT as the directing authority.

12. 57th GST Council Meeting

8 October 2026

  • The 57th GST Council meeting was chaired by the Union Finance Minister, Smt. Nirmala Sitharaman.
  • Proposals: general penalty cut from ₹25,000 to ₹10,000; prosecution threshold raised from ₹1 crore to ₹5 crore; show-cause notices only above ₹10,000.
  • The Council proposed removing GST officers’ arrest powers; criminal prosecution remains available for offences under GST law.
  • The reforms are planned for implementation from 1 April 2027, after the necessary legal amendments.
Exam angle: Remember the three numbers: ₹25,000 to ₹10,000; ₹1 crore to ₹5 crore; implementation from 1 April 2027.

13. Cap on Trade Margins of Anti-Cancer Drugs

Notified in October 2026

  • The National Pharmaceutical Pricing Authority (NPPA), under the Ministry of Chemicals and Fertilizers, capped trade margins at 30% for 137 non-scheduled anti-cancer drugs.
  • Prices fall by up to 96.45% across more than 1,000 formulations; 1,031 of 1,225 formulations get cheaper.
  • The estimated saving for patients is ₹3,502.31 crore a year; revised maximum retail prices were set to apply from 9 November 2026.
  • The move followed the Supreme Court’s scrutiny of mark-ups by hospitals and distributors.
Exam angle: Remember the 30% cap on trade margins, 137 drugs and the NPPA as the authority.

19. Retirement Age of District Judicial Officers

Reported 26 October 2026

  • By October 2026, 14 states had agreed in principle to raise the retirement age of district judicial officers from 60 to 62 years.
  • The Supreme Court bench of CJI Surya Kant, Justice Joymalya Bagchi and Justice V. Mohana had issued the direction in September 2026.
  • The first seven states were Chhattisgarh, Karnataka, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Sikkim, Tamil Nadu and West Bengal.
  • Officers reaching 60 must pass a High Court suitability and performance assessment to continue; the Court rejected the plea of financial burden.
Exam angle: Remember 60 to 62 years and the 14 states. The original direction was given in September.
