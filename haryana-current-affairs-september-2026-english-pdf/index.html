2. Polity & Governance

A. Monsoon Session, Haryana Vidhan Sabha (27 Aug – 1 Sep 2026)

  • Session began 27 August 2026 with ‘Vande Mataram’; Congress MLAs wore black and alleged police excesses, causing a 20-minute adjournment (Day 1). • Business Advisory Committee met 26 Aug under Speaker Harvinder Kalyan; members included CM Nayab Singh Saini, Energy Minister Anil Vij, Parliamentary Affairs Minister Mahipal Dhanda, Leader of Opposition Bhupinder Singh Hooda. Deputy Speaker: Dr Krishan Lal Middha. • On the final day (1 September), 11 Bills were passed after discussion (table below). • Separately reported as passed during the session (single source – verify): Haryana Courts (Amendment) Bill 2026 (Act 1918); Haryana Municipal (Amendment) Bill 2026 (Act 1973); Haryana Municipal Corporation (Amendment) Bill 2026 (Act 1994); Haryana Baba Shri Khatu Shyam Chulkana Dham Shrine Bill, 2026. • The Right to Business Bill was later notified as the Haryana Right to Business Act, 2026 (reported 22 Sep): time-bound approvals and regulatory relief for eligible MSME manufacturing units; units must keep obtaining statutory approvals through the Invest Haryana Single Window Portal during the moratorium.

B. The 11 Bills passed on 1 September 2026

#BillKey provisions / purpose
1Haryana Appropriation (No. 3) Bill, 2026Authorises ₹7,136.63 crore from the Consolidated Fund for FY 2026-27 (supplementary spending).
2Haryana State Commission for Minorities Bill, 2026Gives statutory status to the State Minorities Commission: evaluates minority development, monitors safeguards, inquires into complaints of deprivation of rights, studies socio-economic/educational status.
3Haryana Village Common Lands (Regulation) Amendment Bill, 2026Amends 1961 Act. Power to approve sale of Shamlat Deh land by Gram Panchayats to those who built houses on it on or before 31 Mar 2004 shifted from Director, Development & Panchayats to the Deputy Commissioner.
4Haryana Local Audit Bill, 2026Statutory framework for audit of local bodies (PRIs, ULBs), state universities, BSEH Bhiwani, Maharaja Agrasen Medical College, govt/aided schools etc. Director, Local Audit can issue surcharge notices; statutory time limit for annual audit reports to Legislature.
5Haryana Private Universities (Amendment) Bill, 2026Amends 2006 Act; paves way for Kusum International University (Rewari) and Masters Union University (Gurugram); linked to NEP target of 50% GER by 2030.
6Haryana State Commission for Women (Amendment) Bill, 2026Non-official members raised from 5 to 7 (Act 2012). Replaces the Ordinance promulgated by the Governor on 9 July 2026.
7Haryana GST (Amendment) Bill, 2026Aligns with Finance Act 2026 (Central Act No. 4 of 2026): post-sale discounts (s.15(3)), credit notes (s.34), provisional refund for inverted duty structure (s.54(6)).
8Haryana Panchayati Raj (Amendment) Bill, 2026Gram Sabha quorum for approving beneficiaries/GPDP cut from 40%/30%/20% to 20% (main), 15% (first adjourned), prescribed number (second adjourned).
9Haryana State Council for Physiotherapy (Repeal) Bill, 2026Repeals the 2020 Act; regulation moves to Haryana State Allied and Healthcare Council under the central NCAH Professions Act, 2021.
10Haryana Law Officers (Engagement) Amendment Bill, 2026Amends 2016 Act. Specially qualified advocates under s.6(3) proviso raised from 10 to 15, citing the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023.
11Haryana Right to Business Bill, 2026Self-certification, in-principle pre-establishment approvals, deemed approval if no decision in time, 36-month moratorium to obtain statutory approvals, limited inspections, appeals and nodal agencies.

4. Economy, Industry & Energy

A. GST performance

  • Haryana’s gross GST collection was ₹10,097 crore in September 2026, 5th among states (highlighted by CM at the 57th GST Council meeting, Bharat Mandapam; reported 14 Oct). • ~1.3% of India’s area and ~2% of population but over 7% of domestic GST revenue. • State GST revenue: ₹39,743 crore (2024-25) → ₹48,289 crore (2025-26), +22%. First six months of FY 2026-27: ₹29,108 crore vs ₹23,058 crore.

B. MSME incentives released – 10 Sep 2026

  • First tranche of ₹311.25 crore to 670 beneficiaries via e-Kuber (CM press conference, Haryana Niwas). Budget promise: 50% of eligible incentive within 7 working days of applying on the Invest Haryana portal; balance within 45 working days after scrutiny.

  • Prudential Financial Innovation Centre India – Prudential Financial Inc.’s first innovation centre in India – opened at Cyber City, Gurugram under Haryana’s GCC policy; inaugurated 21 Sep by Industries & Commerce Minister Rao Narbir Singh. • Maruti Suzuki commissioned its first green hydrogen plant – 300 kW electrolyser – at Manesar (pilot, surplus solar power; possible scale-up in Haryana and Gujarat). • Finance laws of the month: Appropriation (No. 3) Bill ₹7,136.63 crore; GST Amendment; Right to Business Act.

  • BharatNet: 5,154 of Haryana’s 6,222 gram panchayats are now connected under the BharatNet initiative. • Panchkula Waterfront: Panchkula Municipal Corporation and HSVP announced a joint venture to build an artificial lake and waterfront tourism hub in Panchkula.

5. Agriculture & Environment

A. Kharif procurement (KMS 2026-27)

  • Centre approved on 28 Sep the start of procurement in Haryana from 29 Sep instead of 1 Oct (state requests of 15 and 23 Sep). • Policy (notified 22 Jul): Aadhaar-based biometric verification; GPS tracking via AIS-140 VLTS; MSP payment in 48–72 hours; twice-daily auctions; tighter norms for rice millers; District Milling Committees under DCs. Agriculture Minister: Shyam Singh Rana. • Static link: procurement is from farmers registered on Meri Fasal Mera Byora.

C. CM review of farm & cooperative schemes (28 Sep 2026)

  • Linking IFMS (Integrated Fertiliser Management System) with Meri Fasal Mera Byora saved ₹820 crore subsidy (8 Oct 2025 – 14 Sep 2026) and curbed black-marketing of urea/DAP. • CM ordered an e-Pest Surveillance System, field visits by agri-scientists and a research centre for improved crop varieties. • Officials told to explore ₹10/litre subsidy to gaushalas under the Mukhyamantri Dugdh Utpadak Protsahan Yojana; HAFED, Sugarfed and HDDCF reviewed. • 8 Sep: statewide ₹1,000/acre incentive via DBT for green manuring, crop diversification and water conservation.
