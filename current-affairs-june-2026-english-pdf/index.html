Haryana Housing Board Scheme 2026

Context / Why in News?

The Haryana government released a fresh batch of residential plots across three of the state's most in-demand urban centres, continuing its push to widen access to affordable housing for lower and middle-income households.

Key Highlights

  • About 580 plots released across Panchkula, Gurugram, and Faridabad, giving applicants a choice across the state's premium real-estate corridor. • Plot sizes range from 42.25 to 142.71 sq m, with prices starting as low as Rs 18 lakh, making the scheme accessible across different income brackets. • The scheme specifically targets the Economically Weaker Section (EWS), Low Income Group (LIG), and Middle Income Group (MIG) categories. • Special reservation quotas have been built in for senior citizens, women applicants, and differently-abled persons.

Haryana One Time Settlement (OTS) Scheme, 2026

Context / Why in News?

The Haryana government issued detailed FAQs to help long-pending taxpayers settle outstanding dues dating back to the pre-GST era, clearing a legacy backlog of tax disputes.

Key Highlights

  • Covers all outstanding dues up to 30 June 2017 arising under seven separate pre-GST tax laws, including the Haryana Value Added Tax Act 2003, the Central Sales Tax Act 1956, and the Haryana General Sales Tax Act, 1973 (not to be confused with GST, which did not exist until 2017). • Waivers on outstanding dues range from 35% up to a full 100%, depending on the size of the pending dues. • Taxpayers can clear dues as a single lump-sum payment or across three instalments, once a provisional settlement order is issued.

Exam Insight / Static Takeaway

Tests understanding of the pre-GST to GST transition and the broader category of legacy tax dispute resolution mechanisms many states have rolled out.
