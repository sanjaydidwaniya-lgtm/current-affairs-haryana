About Me

Welcome to my personal and professional hub! I am Sanjay, hailing from the historic town of Loharu in the Bhiwani district of Haryana.

Since 2019, I have been proudly serving as a Group D employee of the Haryana Government. Balancing my dedicated public service with a relentless pursuit of knowledge, I hold double postgraduate degrees (in History and Chemistry) from Vardhman Mahaveer Open University (VMOU), Kota.

My journey is driven by a deep commitment to continuous learning, administrative excellence, and empowering fellow aspirants. Whether it is navigating the intricacies of regional agricultural administration, preparing for advanced academic milestones like the HCS preparation, or building digital platforms and educational resources to support students and government job seekers, I believe in steady dedication, hard work, and community growth.

Thank you for visiting my platform and being part of this journey!
