Operation Sindoor — 1-Year Analysis

Source: PIB official releases | Indian Army press briefings | ORF Special Report No.299 (March 2026) | Employment News 16-22 May 2026

Note: Military casualty/asset figures below are India's OFFICIAL STATED CLAIMS per Army press briefings. Present them as such in exam answers.

9 Terror Sites Struck (PIB)100+ Terrorists Killed (India's Claim)13 Pak Aircraft Destroyed (Claimed)11 Airfields Hit (Claimed)88 hrs Conflict Duration 7-10 May 2025

Background & Trigger

  • Pahalgam Terror Attack (22 April 2025): 26 civilians killed — all male, mostly Hindu tourists, in targeted point-blank executions in Baisaran meadow. India attributed "cross-border linkages" to Pakistan-based groups (LeT/JeM).
  • Operation Launch: Night of 6-7 May 2025. PIB statement released at 1:44 AM IST on 7 May 2025 confirming the operation had commenced.
  • Weapons Used: Long-range stand-off weapons, air-launched cruise missiles, loitering munitions (kamikaze-style drones that loiter before striking).
  • Targets: Nine sites linked to Lashkar-e-Taiba (LeT) and Jaish-e-Mohammed (JeM) in Pakistan and Pakistan-Occupied Jammu & Kashmir (PoJK).
  • Duration: 88 hours (7-10 May 2025) — most significant India-Pakistan military confrontation since Kargil War (1999).

Escalation & Ceasefire

  • Pakistan launched Operation Bunyan-un-Marsoos as counter-offensive — India claims it was largely thwarted.
  • Night of 9-10 May 2025: Pakistan allegedly fired four missiles towards New Delhi — all reportedly intercepted by India's air defence.
  • India struck key Pakistan Air Force (PAF) bases including Nur Khan, Rafiqui and Murid airbases — unprecedented depth of cross-border strikes.
  • US diplomatic intervention (Secretary of State Marco Rubio + Vice President JD Vance) led to ceasefire on afternoon of 10 May 2025.
  • India's S-400 Triumf air defence system (procured from Russia) credited with protecting Indian airspace.
  • BrahMos supersonic cruise missile (~$5 million/unit, Indo-Russian co-development) used extensively. New BrahMos integration facility opened post-Sindoor — expanding production for domestic use and exports (Vietnam, Indonesia are buyers).

Assam — First North-Eastern State to Pass Uniform Civil Code

GS-2 | Polity | Constitutional Law | Governance

Source: Employment News 6-12 Jun 2026

  • Assam Legislative Assembly passed the Uniform Civil Code (UCC) Bill.
  • First state in the Northeast — third in the country to adopt UCC (after Goa and Uttarakhand).
  • Establishes common civil legal framework governing marriage, divorce, succession and live-in relationships.
  • Scheduled Tribes excluded from its scope — to preserve constitutional protections and customary practices.
  • Monogamy mandatory under the Bill; minimum marriage age = 21 years (grooms), 18 years (brides).
  • Marriages may continue to be solemnised through existing religious ceremonies and traditional customs.
  • Aims: Legal uniformity, gender justice, replace religion-based personal laws with a common framework.

Exam Tip: UCC = Article 44 (DPSP). Goa: UCC since Portuguese civil code era. Uttarakhand: Passed UCC in February 2024.

VB-G RAM G Act 2025 — MGNREGA Replacement (Effective 1 July 2026)

  • Guaranteed employment: 125 days per household per year (vs 100 days under MGNREGA — 25 extra days added).

  • Federalism concern: Shift from demand-driven (MGNREGA) to more top-down allocation model.
