India's First Fully Digital Census Commences (April 1, 2026)

India launched its first fully digital Census on April 1, 2026 — the 16th Census overall and 8th since Independence. Phase 1

(House Listing) began April 1, 2026. Phase 2 (Population Enumeration, including caste) is scheduled for February 2027. Snow-bound regions use October 1, 2026 as the reference date. The exercise uses geo-tagging for accuracy. A

Self-Enumeration portal (launched across 8 States/UTs) lets citizens fill data online. Two mascots — Pragati and Vikas —

were unveiled. Caste enumeration returns after 1931, a gap of 96 years. Total outlay: Rs 11,700+ crore. Data security is

governed under the Census Act.

KEY FACTS:

Amaravati Declared Andhra Pradesh's Permanent Capital

Parliament passed the Andhra Pradesh Reorganisation (Amendment) Bill, 2026, granting statutory recognition to Amaravati as the permanent capital. The Rajya Sabha cleared it on April 2, 2026, after the Lok Sabha had already passed it. The bill follows a 2025 resolution of the AP Legislative Assembly. The capital question had been disputed since AP's bifurcation in 2014 under the AP Reorganisation Act. The Jaganmohan Reddy government's earlier three-capital proposal — Amaravati (Legislative), Kurnool (Judicial), Visakhapatnam (Executive) — was abandoned.

KEY FACTS:

  • Bill: AP Reorganisation (Amendment) Bill, 2026 • Rajya Sabha passed: April 2, 2026 • AP bifurcated in 2014 — Telangana carved out • Earlier 3-capital proposal abandoned

PFBR Kalpakkam Achieves Criticality — Nuclear Milestone

India's 500 MW Prototype Fast Breeder Reactor (PFBR) at Kalpakkam, Tamil Nadu achieved first criticality — the point at which a nuclear chain reaction becomes self-sustaining. This advances India's three-stage nuclear programme designed by Dr. Homi Bhabha. Stage 1 uses natural uranium in PHWRs. Stage 2 uses plutonium in Fast Breeder Reactors like PFBR. Stage 3 will harness thorium as fuel. India holds the world's third-largest thorium reserves. PFBR is designed and built by BHAVINI (Bharatiya Nabhikiya Vidyut Nigam Limited). India also announced a target of 100 GW nuclear capacity by 2047 (current: 8.8 GW).

KEY FACTS:

  • Location: Kalpakkam, Tamil Nadu | Capacity: 500 MW • Organisation: BHAVINI • India's 3-stage plan: PHWRs (Stage 1) >> FBRs (Stage 2) >> Thorium (Stage 3) • India has world's 3rd largest thorium reserves • Nuclear target: 100 GW by 2047 (current: 8.8 GW)

(a) NPCIL (b) BARC (c) BHAVINI (d) ISRO Ans: (c) BHAVINI

Nari Shakti Vandan Adhiniyam Comes Into Force (April 16, 2026)

The Nari Shakti Vandan Adhiniyam — the 106th Constitutional Amendment Act, 2023 — came into effect on April 16, 2026. It mandates 33% reservation for women in the Lok Sabha, the Delhi Legislative Assembly, and all State Legislative Assemblies. The reservation will activate after the next delimitation exercise, which requires fresh Census data. A sub-reservation for SC/ST women is carved within the 33% quota. Minister Kiren Rijiju described the notification as an ideological milestone for women's political empowerment. The bill was originally passed in a special Parliament session in September 2023.

KEY FACTS:

  • 106th Constitutional Amendment Act, 2023 • 33% reservation: Lok Sabha + all State Assemblies + Delhi Assembly • Effective after delimitation post next Census • Sub-reservation for SC/ST women within the 33% • Passed in Special Parliament Session, September 2023

Supreme Court: Fundamental Right Against Climate Change Impacts

The Supreme Court of India ruled that citizens have a fundamental right to be protected from the adverse impacts of climate change, linking it explicitly to Article 21 (Right to Life and Personal Liberty). The bench held that the right to a clean environment and stable climate is intrinsic to the right to life with dignity. This landmark judgment expands India's environmental jurisprudence and is the first explicit recognition of climate rights by the Supreme Court. It will have far-reaching consequences for climate litigation and government policy-making on emissions, industry regulation, and urban planning.

KEY FACTS:

  • Article 21 (Right to Life) extended to cover climate protection • First explicit recognition of climate rights by India's Supreme Court • Will impact future climate litigation and environmental policy

Great Indian Bustard Chick Born in Kutch, Gujarat

A critically endangered Great Indian Bustard (GIB) chick was born in Abdasa, Kutch — a landmark for conservation. Since eggs in Kutch are frequently unfertilised, an egg was transported from Rajasthan to Kutch in a portable incubator over a 19-hour journey, placed in a wild female's nest on March 22, and hatched on March 26. The GIB (Ardeotis nigriceps) is found only in India and is the state bird of Rajasthan. Wild population has fallen below 150, threatened by power lines and habitat loss. In a related development, four cheetah cubs were born at Kuno National Park, Sheopur (MP) — the first wild birth by an Indian-born female cheetah since the reintroduction project began in September 2022.

KEY FACTS:

  • GIB (Ardeotis nigriceps): Critically Endangered | State bird of Rajasthan • Wild GIB population: fewer than 150 • Egg transported Rajasthan to Kutch in portable incubator (19 hours) • Cheetah reintroduction: Namibia (Sep 2022) + South Africa (Feb 2023) • Cheetahs declared extinct in India: 1952 | 4 cubs born at Kuno NP, Sheopur, MP

Science, Environment & Tech

Memristors: Energy-Efficient AI Computing Breakthrough (Cambridge)

Researchers at Cambridge University developed a stable hafnium-oxide-based memristor (memory-resistor). A memristor is an electronic component that regulates current flow while retaining memory of past electrical states — even when powered off. The key breakthrough: memristors merge memory and processing in one unit, eliminating the Von Neumann Bottleneck — the energy lost by constantly shuttling data between separate CPU and RAM in traditional computers. This can reduce AI computing energy consumption by up to 70%. As AI models grow exponentially, data centre energy consumption has become a global concern, making this breakthrough strategically significant.

KEY FACTS:

  • Developed at: Cambridge University | Material: hafnium-oxide • Merges memory + processing in one unit • Eliminates Von Neumann Bottleneck (CPU-RAM energy waste) • Energy saving potential: up to 70% reduction in AI computing energy • Considered the 4th fundamental circuit element

Q. The Von Neumann Bottleneck refers to:

(a) Overheating of CPUs (b) Energy wasted in CPU-RAM data transfer (c) Slow internet connectivity (d) Battery drain in mobile devices Ans: (b) Energy wasted in CPU-RAM data transfer

South Atlantic Anomaly (SAA) — Earth's Magnetic Weak Zone

New data from the ESA's Swarm satellite constellation recorded significant developments in the South Atlantic Anomaly (SAA) — a large region over South America and southwest Africa where Earth's magnetic field is substantially weaker than normal. Often called the Bermuda Triangle of Space, the weakened shielding allows cosmic rays and energetic solar particles to penetrate closer to Earth's surface, triggering anomalies in satellites and spacecraft passing through. The SAA is slowly moving westward and expanding. It is jointly monitored by NOAA and ESA.

KEY FACTS:

  • SAA: Region over South America and southwest Africa • Called the 'Bermuda Triangle of Space' • Monitored by: ESA's Swarm satellite constellation • Effect: Satellite malfunctions in the SAA zone • Slowly moving westward and expanding

AMOC Slowdown — Atlantic Ocean Conveyor Belt Warning

New oceanographic data confirmed a steady weakening of the Atlantic Meridional Overturning Circulation (AMOC) — the ocean current system that transports warm water northward and cold water southward across the Atlantic. AMOC is often called the ocean conveyor belt or Great Ocean Conveyor. Potential consequences of collapse: Northern Europe cools significantly (paradoxically, despite global warming); the Southern Ocean flips from a carbon sink to a carbon source; global weather patterns are severely disrupted. The primary driver of weakening is freshwater influx from melting Greenland glaciers, reducing ocean salinity and density.

KEY FACTS:

  • AMOC = Atlantic Meridional Overturning Circulation • Functions as ocean conveyor belt — critical for global climate regulation • Weakening cause: Greenland ice melt >> freshwater dilutes salinity • Risk: N. Europe cooling; Southern Ocean becomes carbon source • Monitored via deep ocean bottom pressure sensors

Q. AMOC stands for:

(a) Atlantic Marine Ocean Current (b) Atlantic Meridional Overturning Circulation (c) Arctic Meridional Ocean Circulation (d) Atlantic Mesopelagic Overturning Current Ans: (b) Atlantic Meridional Overturning Circulation

April South Asia Heatwave — Human-Caused Climate Change

A rapid scientific assessment by World Weather Attribution and ClimaMeter found that human-induced climate change made the intense mid-to-late April 2026 heatwave across India and Pakistan approximately 3 times more likely to occur. Temperatures peaked near 47.6 degrees C in parts of Uttar Pradesh, with several cities recording approximately 45 degrees C — nearly 8 degrees C above normal seasonal averages. The 15-day event was classified as super-rapid intensification. IUCN also updated its Red List, moving Emperor Penguins and Antarctic Fur Seals to more critical threatened categories due to rapid Antarctic sea ice loss.

KEY FACTS:

  • Peak temperature: 47.6 deg C (Uttar Pradesh) — 8 deg C above normal • Climate change made this heatwave 3 times more likely (WWA + ClimaMeter) • Duration: 15-day super-rapid intensification event • IUCN Red List update: Emperor Penguin + Antarctic Fur Seal moved to critical categories

Generic Oral Semaglutide Approved in India (CDSCO)

India's CDSCO (Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation) approved generic versions of oral Semaglutide — a GLP-1 receptor agonist (glucagon-like peptide-1 receptor agonist). Semaglutide treats Type-2 Diabetes and Obesity. The branded versions — Ozempic (diabetes) and Wegovy (obesity) by Novo Nordisk — cost Rs 10,000-15,000 per month in India. Generic versions will drastically lower costs and widen access. GLP-1 agonists work by mimicking a gut hormone that stimulates insulin release and suppresses appetite. India approved Kankaria Coaching Depot, Ahmedabad as India's first Water Neutral Railway Depot (uses phytoremediation).

KEY FACTS:

  • CDSCO = Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation (India's drug regulator) • Drug class: GLP-1 receptor agonist | Uses: Type-2 Diabetes + Obesity • Brand names: Ozempic (diabetes), Wegovy (obesity) — by Novo Nordisk • Kankaria Coaching Depot, Ahmedabad: India's first Water Neutral Railway Depot • Phytoremediation: using plants to treat wastewater
