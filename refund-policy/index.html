Refund Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026.

Current Affairs Haryana sells digital study material in PDF format. Payments are taken on Instamojo, a third-party payment service. Please read this policy before you buy.

How delivery works

After a successful payment, Instamojo gives you the PDF download link on the confirmation page and sends it to the email address you used at checkout. You can download the file right away. Please check that the PDF opens correctly as soon as you receive it.

No refund after a successful download

A PDF is a digital product that cannot be returned once it has been delivered. Because of this, we do not offer refunds if you change your mind, bought the wrong month or language, or no longer need the file. Please check the month and the language (Hindi or English) on the product page before you pay. You can also read the full free post for the same month on this site before buying.

When you are eligible for a refund or replacement

  • You paid, but did not receive the download link or the file.
  • The file is corrupted or does not open, and we cannot fix it by sending a new copy.
  • You were charged twice for the same product.
  • The wrong file was delivered, for example a different month or language than the one you ordered.

In the first, second and fourth cases, we will first send you the correct file. If we cannot fix the problem, we will refund the amount.

How to ask for help

Email us at contact@currentaffairsharyana.in within 7 days of the payment. Please include:

  • Your name and the email address used at checkout.
  • Your Instamojo payment ID or order ID.
  • The product name (month and language).
  • A short description of the problem, with a screenshot if possible.

Refund timeline

We aim to reply within 2 to 3 working days. An approved refund is sent back to the original payment method through Instamojo. The time it takes to show in your account depends on your bank or UPI app and is usually 5 to 7 working days.

Cases we cannot refund

  • Requests made more than 7 days after the payment.
  • Problems that come from your device, such as a PDF reader that cannot open the file. A free reader such as Google Drive or Adobe Acrobat Reader will open it.
  • Files that were shared, resold or posted publicly by the buyer. These break our Terms of Use.
  • Differences of opinion about the content or style, as long as the file matches what the product page describes.

Corrections

If you find a factual mistake in a PDF, please tell us. We update our files and will share the corrected version with the buyer.

Questions: contact@currentaffairsharyana.in. See also our Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.
